Gameplay

Scenarios

The game is designed as a series of scenarios divided into a prologue section and 3 chapters. Players engage with the scenario by answering a series of questions. As the player progresses through the game the can add to their score or subtract from their score.

In certain scenarios, the wrong choice will end the game.

Game Advancement

To advance the game, players must select from a series of options or input text into the appropriate field. Each answer will present a result that will be displayed in a popup.

Players cannot return to the scenario and select a different answer after a selection is made, with the exception of the input fields.
