# The Forgotten File
Play [The Forgotten File: The Journey to the Castle of Preservation](https://archivistamanda.github.io/the-forgotten-file/)

---
*The Forgotten File: The Journey to the Castle of Preservation* was developed in recognition of World Digital Preservation Day 2026 and its theme, [Save Our Stories (SOS): Our Digital Heritage in Danger.](https://www.dpconline.org/events/world-digital-preservation-day) Through the story of a lost and forgotten file embarking on a journey to the Castle of Preservation, this narrative seeks to raise awareness of the importance of safeguarding our digital heritage and the stories, knowledge, and memories it contains. In an increasingly digital world, files and records can easily become lost, inaccessible, corrupted, or forgotten as technologies evolve and become obsolete.

By personifying a digital file and portraying its journey toward preservation, the story highlights the importance of context and the crucial role it plays in determining preservation pathways. It equally conveys the challenges and significance of protecting digital information for future generations. Ultimately, it reminds us that digital files have a story worth saving and that preserving our digital heritage is a shared responsibility.

## Gameplay
![The scenario the mirror within from chapter one](/assets/images/about_images/scenario-screenshot.png)
The game is a choice-driven, text-based adventure in which players guide a forgotten file through a mysterious digital world. Through narrative passages, interactive decisions, and branching scenarios, players uncover clues about the protagonist's identity and explore themes of digital preservation, memory, and data corruption.

Gameplay revolves around reading the story and selecting responses to the situations the protagonist encounters. Each scenario presents the player with a set of choices, ranging from investigating unfamiliar objects to interacting with mysterious computer systems and deciding how to respond to dangerous situations. The consequences of these decisions are communicated through narrative feedback, allowing the story to react to the player's actions.

In addition to selecting predefined choices, players occasionally interact with the game through text input. For example, they may need to enter a specific response to satisfy a requirement and unlock the next part of the narrative. These interactions add variety to the gameplay and reinforce the protagonist's direct engagement with the digital environment.

## Scoring
Player decisions also influence a numerical scoring system. Choices can award or deduct points, rewarding certain actions while penalizing others. Maintaining a sufficient score (above 1) is essential to achieving the game's completion condition, and poor decisions can lead to a game-over scenario. This system encourages players to consider the potential consequences of their actions as they progress through the story.

## Memory Profile
The player’s memory profile will update as they progress through the game and gather information. A notice will appear to let the player know their memory profile was new information. The player may interact with the memory profile at any time by clicking the **Memory Profile** button located at the upper left corner of each scenario.
